Parse a declaration source once per text

extractByLiteral parsed the whole source twice per call (its own parse
and findLiteralSites') and every case of declaration.test.ts parsed
the same 5.4 MB chunk again. One extraction took 3.4 s, and under load
the case crossed bun test's 5 s default.

A single-entry cache keyed on the text serves both functions. The
contract counts parses, not wall time. Annulment: disabling the cache
hit drops exactly the two same-text cases; the different-text case
holds. One extraction takes 2.1 s, and both declaration files run in
2.65 s instead of 8.2 s.
