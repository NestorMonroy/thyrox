Re-export canonical same-package symbols consumers ask for

namedImports.ts listed 354 names that a module is asked for and does not
export; 42 of them are defined in another module of the same package.
This re-exports the canonical ones, and nine cross-package types through
keys their packages already declare.

Seven candidates stay out, and the reason is measured, not a style
call. Six came from agent/internal/queryRuntime.ts and headlessRuntime,
bridges toward host bindings typed with the generic AgentMessage: with
them the cli ratchet grew by 71, 70 of them in AttachmentMessage.tsx
reading fields of a record typed unknown. The seventh, Attachment from
loop/transcript.ts, shares the name of the attachment union and not its
shape. getDefaultAppState also stays out: re-exporting it from
AppState.tsx loaded AppStateCompat eagerly and broke the lazy shim that
three suites protect (6 fails with it, 24 pass without it); its single
consumer now imports it from AppStateStore.js, where it is canonical.

Missing names drop from 354 to 292; the cli ratchet drops from 2407 to
2387.
