Port the diff display and line counting from the binary

diff.ts declared a partial port: countLinesChanged, getPatchFromContents
and getPatchForDisplay depended on the diff library and four packages
that did not exist here then. They exist now, and eleven red test files
failed to load on the missing names.

The contract follows the 2.1.275 block that opens with R4e=3,Cxe=5000:
escape & and $ before diffing, rawText, convertTabs and singleHunk, and
edits applied over escaped text with a replacement function. The
binary passes the model as countLinesChanged's second argument; this
tree's callers use the reference form, and adopting the binary's order
would read their file contents as a model name, so the reference form
stays and the counter goes without the model label.

Annulments: counting whole contents without a patch drops exactly its
three cases. Either dollar guard alone drops nothing, because escaping
and the replacement function cover each other; removing both drops
exactly the dollar case, and the test says so.
