Port 2.1.275 tool-rule matching and getAssistantMessageText

The permission engine that twelve red suites wait on (oT and Sjn in
chunk-q2gh92k2.js) names tools the 2.1.275 way, which this tree did
not: toolRuleMatching.ts reimplements it.

- permissionToolName (ag): MCP tools as mcp__server__tool with both
  names normalized; claude.ai servers collapse their underscores.
- toolMatchesRuleName ($r): exact name, family parent, whole MCP
  server or a wildcard in its tool name, and, when asked, a wildcard
  in the rule name and alias expansion.
- matchesWholeToolRule, getDenyRuleForToolCall, getAskRuleForToolCall
  (Rs, mG, ws, ld): rules without content, aliases expanded only for
  settings rules, EndConversation outside the rules.
- matchingFieldRule (Ah): field:value rules with wildcards, through
  aliases and the family parent; the tool's own content field is left
  to its own checker, and device reads _host when absent.

getAssistantMessageText (qO) joins an assistant message's non-empty
text blocks.

Two package-graph suites turned red with the previous commits:
permission now declares ignore and @thyrox/memory, with the lock
regenerated by bun install, and pathSafety uses the managedPath key
@thyrox/config exports.

Annulment: without the settings-only alias gate, without excluding
the tool's own content field and without the EndConversation
exemption, exactly one case fails each.
