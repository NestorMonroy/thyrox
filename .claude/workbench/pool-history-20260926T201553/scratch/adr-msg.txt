Record ADR-THYROX-002: the pool owns its cycle

The run-measure-adjust cycle around bin/headless-pool lived only in
tsc_cycle.py, so any other caller of the pool lost it silently and
tsc_cycle fixed --memfree 3G against a measured item peak of about
260 MB.

The decision moves the cycle into the pool: a per-template history
derives the cache TTL through choose_cache_ttl, with its three
branches, and --memfree from the measured peak plus a named reserve
for a neighbour. Declared values always win, and without history
nothing is invented. It also records why the derivation is Python
called from the shell, and why VRAM is out of scope.

Implemented in thyrox@7888bff3.
