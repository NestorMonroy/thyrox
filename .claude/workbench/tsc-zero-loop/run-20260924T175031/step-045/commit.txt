Declare the update channel and view settings keys

The settings schema lacked autoUpdatesChannel, minimumVersion and
defaultView, so reading them fell through the passthrough index
signature as unknown. Every entry of the /config menu that showed one
of them typed its value as {} and poisoned the Setting union for the
whole list. The three keys now carry the schemas of 2.1.275:
latest/stable/rc, a version string, and chat/transcript.
