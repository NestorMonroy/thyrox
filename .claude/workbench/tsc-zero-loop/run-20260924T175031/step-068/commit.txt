Type the AgentMessage boundary from its readers

AgentMessage stays a model of its own, separate from Message, as the
executor directed. qwen-code keeps its AgentMessage apart from the API
type the same way. A type-checker census of what the loop reads showed
that two fields contradicted their own readers. message.content was
declared unknown[] but read after typeof === 'string', and
message.usage was declared { [k]: number } but cast by its only
reader. With those two fixed, the canonical Message satisfies
AgentMessage structurally, as the header of internalTypes.ts promises,
and the six inbound call sites compile without casts.

countToolCalls goes back to a narrow type of its own, as in the
source, with a guard that actually narrows; step 065 had tied it to
Message. toCoreMessages and fromCoreMessages recover the typed
signatures of the source instead of a generic identity, which named no
boundary.

CoreMessage from coreMessages.ts where AgentCore expects the one from
agentMessages.ts.
