Port the stream handler and real stream message shapes

The REPL, the remote session and the agent hooks import
handleMessageFromStream, StreamingToolUse, StreamingThinking and
hasSuccessfulToolCall from messages.js, and none of them existed.
They are now implemented natively with the positional contract of
the source (ccnmt v2.1.88, utils/messages.ts): stream events drive
the spinner mode, the streamed tool uses, the thinking and text
previews; complete messages are delivered, except tombstones, which
retract, and SDK tool summaries, which the REPL ignores.

That handler needs to tell those items apart, and it could not:
TombstoneMessage and ToolUseSummaryMessage were aliases of Message,
and StreamEvent and RequestStartEvent were open shapes, so no
discriminant narrowed. They now declare their literal type and their
fields. That also fits createToolUseSummaryMessage, whose result did
not fit Message, and runAgent's recordable-message guard now takes
the minimal shape it reads instead of a union of those aliases.
