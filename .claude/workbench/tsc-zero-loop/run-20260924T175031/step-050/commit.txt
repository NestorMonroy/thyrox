Scan the Chrome bridge sockets asynchronously

The ported getAllSocketPaths reads the socket directory and is async,
as in 2.1.275, but the vendored socket pool typed getSocketPaths as
synchronous and called it without awaiting. The pool now awaits the
scan the way 2.1.275 does: concurrent ensureConnected calls share one
scan in flight, and a scan that throws is logged instead of breaking
the connection. The generation and orphaned-client tracking of
2.1.275 is not ported.

Annulled in turn, the shared refresh and the scan-failure catch each
dropped exactly their test.
