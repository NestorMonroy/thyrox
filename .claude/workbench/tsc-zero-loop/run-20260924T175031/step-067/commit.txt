internalTypes.ts declared its own minimal message shape (type: string,
content: unknown[]), a second message universe beside messageShapes.ts.
QueryEngine and the loop helpers spoke one or the other, so every
boundary between them failed under strict mode. Steps 063 and 066 tried
to harden QueryEngine alone and were rejected for that reason.

The V7 section 8 reason for the file, not importing app-compat types,
does not cover messageShapes.ts, which lives in this package.
AgentMessage and AgentAssistantMessage are now aliases of the canonical
types, and QueryEngine takes Message, SystemCompactBoundaryMessage and
the SDK message types from their canonical homes instead of local
stubs.
