Give VRAM admission its TypeScript face

The admission lived in Python and shell only, so a TS caller had no
way to reserve in the shared ledger, and one that probed nvidia-smi
on its own would reopen the check-then-use race the ledger closes.

gpuAdmission.ts does not reimplement the protocol: admitVram and
releaseVram launch bin/gpu_monitor, as headless-pool does, and map
its exit code (0 admitted, 3 timeout, anything else an error rather
than a "not admitted"). The cross-language test races a Python and a
TS admission of 3000 MiB with 5000 free: exactly one enters. With the
TS face pointed at a private ledger, only the two cross cases fall.

The background-work rule now says the admission reserves, not just
checks, and names the three faces.
