Reserve VRAM under a shared ledger before launch

Admission compared each item's peak against the free VRAM alone, so
two items that saw 5000 MiB free with 3000 MiB each both started (the
shell probe in the bench reproduced it). Check and reservation now run
under the shared_lock of a committed-VRAM ledger: an item is admitted
when free minus what live owners reserved and do not use yet covers
its peak, and it releases the reservation when it ends. A dead
owner's reservation stops counting on its own.

The pieces stay separate: VramLedger persists, measure measures,
pending and admissible decide, admit only holds the lock and retries.
wait_free is deleted, not kept beside it.

headless-pool now calls bin/gpu_monitor and bin/pool_history instead
of reaching into src/session with PYTHONPATH, and declares the
ledger as HEADLESS_POOL_VRAM_LEDGER. Annulments in the bench: without
release only the empty-ledger case falls; with a private ledger only
the foreign-reservation case and the empty-ledger case fall.
