Export PYTHONPATH from the isolated TypeScript runner

tests/run.sh exports PYTHONPATH, and the derived subset calls
run_ts_isolated.sh directly. Suites that shell out to a Python gate
(compat.test.ts, branchIntegration.test.ts) passed in the full run and
failed in the subset with "No module named 'verify'": measured, 3
fails without the variable and 0 with it, with or without the reach.ts
port.

The runner now exports the provider's src the same way run.sh does.
Its suite gains the case that removes PYTHONPATH from the caller; its
red is persisted in the bench.
