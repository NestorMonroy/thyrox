Port the provider env layer to reach.ts

reach.py reads the provider's .env as a third layer, answering only the
asking clone's *_<SUFFIX> keys (thyrox@400fc454); reach.ts still built
the chain from the process and the clone's own .env, so the TypeScript
side resolved kaupamex-docs' THYROX_*_DOCS keys to nothing.

ProviderEnvFileDeclarations, cloneTopOf and cloneSuffixOf follow the
Python adapter: the provider is the sibling that carries the marker, a
declared THYROX_ENV_FILE turns the layer off, and the same file is
never read as two layers.

Annulments: dropping the suffix filter drops exactly the two leak
cases (the provider's own home, another clone's family); dropping the
THYROX_ENV_FILE guard drops exactly its case. The derived subset of
reach.ts consumers runs 124 pass, 0 fail.
