Add the judgment proposer and net policy to the tsc loop

The tsc zero loop could only judge mechanical proposals: in four runs
the compiler's code fixes got 2 of 55 accepted, none of them a root
cause. Two pieces were missing for the agent-compiler loop.

agent_proposal turns an edit the agent made in the tree into a loop
candidate (one minimal edit per file, its HEAD base, and the targets
the agent names with a pattern over the before log) and puts the
file back, so the step applies it and tsc judges it, not the agent.
Continuation lines of chained messages are skipped; a real log has
them and the first version refused on them.

The net policy (--net) keeps a lone proposal whose targets fall and
whose total falls, and records what it reveals instead of reverting
it: typing a contract uncovers errors tsc could not see before, and
the strict policy reverted exactly those root-cause fixes. The
scheduler counts accepted-net for the proposer.

Annulment: dropping the total condition fails exactly its case;
dropping the net branch fails the four that need it; not restoring
the base fails its one case. The root baseline (3745) is recorded.
