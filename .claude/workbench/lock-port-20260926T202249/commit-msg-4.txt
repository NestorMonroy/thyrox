Put the shared writers under the lock

step_setup.register is read, check, append: with eight concurrent
writers and a slow read injected into the workers, 30 setups left 54
rows. Under the lock of setups.jsonl they leave 30.

step_close is now the only git writer of a step, and it commits and
pushes under a per-repo commit lock that lives in the git directory,
next to git's own index.lock. With another live owner holding it the
close refuses before committing and names that owner, instead of
colliding half way as step 156 did.

pool_history.record stays without the lock on purpose: it is one
append of a line under 4 KB, which Linux writes whole, and no case
could tell the lock apart.

Also replaces the forbidden word "corrida" with "ejecución" in the
files this touches.
