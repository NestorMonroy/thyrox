Translate identifiers that entered after the baseline

The identifier gate counted 245 Spanish identifiers that landed after
its baseline was frozen, across 39 files of src/corpus, src/paths,
src/session and their suites. They are translated word by word with
the previous sweep's token renamer, which never touches strings or
comments; test names get whole English sentences instead, since a
word-by-word gloss of a sentence is not English.

Four words are names of things, not prose, and join the gate's
technical vocabulary: clx and cch (MS-DOC and MS-XLS field names),
bie (INEGI's databank) and sep. posterior joins it too: in
tsc_schedule it is the Bayesian posterior, and translating it to
"later" was a wrong rename caught by that module's own suite.

Eight dict keys stay Spanish and are frozen with their reason: they
are data, the quarter ordinals INEGI publishes and the terms the
census counts. generate_bin.py and its suite stay out by the
standing directive until the rest of the tree is green.

Also declares THYROX_TS_WIDTH, which run_ts_isolated.sh reads, and
regenerates bin/ for the two modules that had no wrapper.
