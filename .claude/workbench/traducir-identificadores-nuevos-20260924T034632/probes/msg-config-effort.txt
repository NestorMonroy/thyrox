Port the effort display from the binary, schemas lazy

Settings schemas: the source and the 2.1.275 binary declare
SettingsSchema, PermissionsSchema and EnvironmentVariablesSchema as
lazy factories, and this tree held plain instances. pluginLoader and
updateConfig already called SettingsSchema() and failed at runtime
with a TypeError; three files declared the divergence instead of
closing it. All three are factories now, and every consumer calls
them. parseSettingSourcesFlag throws the binary's message again. The
suite ported from the reference is aligned to two standing decisions
it contradicted: model takes a full identifier, never an alias, and
includeCoAuthoredBy only admits false; both refusals are now tested.

Effort: seven names the ccnmt port had left out are ported from the
2.1.275 binary (chunk-87qahtf8.js): modelSupportsEffort (I_),
getDefaultEffortForModel (C), resolveModelEffort (Sw),
getDisplayedEffortLevel (TT), getEffortSuffix (Uyt) and both
descriptions (ne, nQn), with the model catalog as capability source
and canonicalModelName (mSe) to match dated or prefixed ids.
modelSupportsMaxEffort and modelSupportsXhighEffort take the binary's
shape (Dj, s3): until now xhigh was refused for Sonnet 5, Opus 5 and
Fable, and a session on claude-fable-5-1 had xhigh clamped to high.
The ccnmt proxy asymmetry for xhigh is not in the measured build, and
its case is realigned. resolvePickerEffortPersistence is not in
2.1.275; it is the earlier API ModelPicker still uses, ported to its
suite's contract. Every guard was annulled on its own and exactly its
cases fell; the hook branch had none and gained one.
