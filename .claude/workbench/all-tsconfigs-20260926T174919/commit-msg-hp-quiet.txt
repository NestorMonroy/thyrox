Write headless-pool timings without the exit line

Without -q, GNU Time puts "Command exited with non-zero status N" before
the measurement of an item that fails. The consumer ai-course-notes
copied the reader into tools/scripts/translation_loop.py and parses the
first word with int(), so a failed chunk -- the one most worth
measuring -- crashes its usage report instead of being counted. The
launchers already pass -q; headless-pool now does too, which protects
any consumer that copied the reader without touching its code.

The fake GNU Time in the suite now behaves like the real one, so the
new case fails without -q and passes with it.
