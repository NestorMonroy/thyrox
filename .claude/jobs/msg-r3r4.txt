Coordinate refresh, cooldowns and windows via the port

ADR-THYROX-006 phases R3 and R4. The provider takes an optional
SharedStateStore; without it every consumer behaves as before.

- Token refresh takes a lease per connection: only the owner calls
  the provider, the others wait for the lease and read the tokens it
  persisted. If the port throws, nothing is refreshed.
- A credential cooldown is published with its TTL, so another proxy
  sees the credential cooled down until it expires.
- checkGlobalWindow counts a request per credential in a shared
  window and reports when the limit is passed.

Annulling the lease drops the two cases that depend on it; skipping
the cooldown publish drops the cross-instance case; counting only
locally drops the two shared-window cases.
