Fix two tests that measured the environment

imageProcessor.test assumed neither sharp nor the native processor was
installed, and asserted rejections. sharp arrived with the
dependencies, so the promises resolved. mock.module cannot simulate
the absence steadily in Bun: a throwing factory fails only the first
import and later ones get the real module. The two loaders are now
replaceable through _setImageImportersForTesting, and the suite makes
both reject. Production behaviour is unchanged. Dropping the fallback
warning fails exactly the two cases that depend on it.

subagent.test compared the harness-created board with the real
store's SQL, which already carries the columns the store adds by
ALTER. src/task/schema.ts declares that floor-not-contract split on
purpose, and tests/task/schema.test.ts pins it. The comparison now
leaves out alterColumns(). Adding the layer columns to the floor
instead was tried and broke those pins, so it was reverted.
