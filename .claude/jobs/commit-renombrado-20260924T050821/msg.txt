Undo a rename applied twice to claude-code literals

A global rename ran twice over "claude-code" and left
"claude-code-how-works-how-works" in 115 files, in 89 forms. Among
them: /etc/claude-code/managed-settings.json, so the managed policy
never loaded; the updater's distribution bucket;
anthropics/claude-code-action; @anthropic-ai/claude-code; the
keybindings schema; and the official plugin marketplace names, whose
absence broke a security boundary. pluginIdentifier.test.ts exposed
it.

The 2.1.275 binary has /etc/claude-code, claude-code-releases,
anthropics/claude-code-action and claude-code-keybindings.json, and
never claude-code-how-works. Every doubled literal becomes
claude-code; none remains. The 340 files citing the
@claude-code-how-works/* package scope as provenance are the
reference project's legitimate name and are left alone. Finding
H-THYROX-171; the bench records the file list.
