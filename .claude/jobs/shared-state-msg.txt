Add @thyrox/shared-state with its port and contract

ADR-THYROX-006 1.1.0 puts the proxy's cross-instance hot state
(leases, windowed counters, TTL values) behind one port. This lands
the port and the contract suite both adapters must pass, so memory
and redis cannot drift in semantics; the adapters come from the R1
and R2 pool items (TASK-THYROX-0267, 0268).

Not @thyrox/coordination: that package is the claims ledger between
sessions. The persistence rule gains the hot-state section.
